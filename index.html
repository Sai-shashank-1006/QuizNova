How to play

Everything runs offline — scores are saved on this device.

AI questions

  • Turn on AI in AI settings and every game gets brand-new questions — they’re hard by default and never repeat ones you’ve had recently.
  • Free: Chrome’s built-in AI runs on your PC (one-time download, no key). Best: Claude, with your own Anthropic API key.
  • Type any topic on the home screen — a series, a game, a player — and get a quiz about it.
  • With AI off, you play the 10 built-in questions per subject, which repeat.
  • AI can still get a fact wrong. On the results screen, press Wrong answer? Report it — the question stops counting against you and won’t come back.
  • Maths doesn’t need AI: by default QuizNova makes brand-new problems every game — from sums to calculus — and checks every answer itself. You can pick AI instead, but AI can get maths answers wrong.

Solo · fastest finger

  • Each question has its own timer. Answer before it runs out.
  • Correct answers score 100 plus up to 100 for speed. Hard AI questions are worth ×1.5, medium ×1.2.
  • Chain correct answers for a streak bonus of up to +100.
  • One of each power-up per game: 50:50 (removes two wrong answers, or gives a hint), +10 s and Skip (keeps your streak).
  • Surprise rounds: Double ×2, Lightning (half the time, ×3) and Mystery box (a secret ×1–×4).
  • Keys: 1–4 or A–D to answer, Enter for the next question.

Battle · 2–4 players, one device

  • Every player logs in with their own ID and gets a buzzer key: Q P Z M.
  • Read the question, then buzz when it says BUZZ! Buzzing early is a false start.
  • The first buzzer answers with 1–4: +100 plus a speed bonus, or −50 if wrong.
  • On touch screens, tap your player card to buzz.

Online room · friends on their own devices

  • Press Online, create a room and send your friends the 5-letter code. They press Online → Join.
  • Everyone answers the same question at the same time, on their own phone or PC. Right answers score 100 plus up to 100 for speed.
  • The host’s device runs the game (and its AI, if you pick AI) — keep it open until the end. Up to 8 players.

Challenge codes · anyone, anywhere

  • After a solo game, press Challenge a friend and send the code.
  • Your friend pastes it into Enter code on their own copy of QuizNova.
  • The code carries the exact questions — even AI ones — so your friend doesn’t need an API key to play them.
